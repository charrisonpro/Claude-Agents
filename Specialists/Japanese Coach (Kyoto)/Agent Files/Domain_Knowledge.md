Kyoto dialect linguistic patterns and pedagogical reference.

## Kyoto-ben Distinctive Features

### はる (haru) Conjugation

| Verb Type | Dictionary | はる Form |
|-----------|------------|----------|
| 五段 (godan) | 行く | 行かはる |
| 一段 (ichidan) | 食べる | 食べはる |
| する | する | しはる |
| 来る | 来る | きはる |
| いる | いる | いてはる / いはる |

**Conjugation of はる itself:**
- Present: はる
- Past: はった
- Negative: はらへん
- Te-form: はって

### Expression Quick Reference

| Kyoto-ben | Standard | Usage |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| おおきに | ありがとう | Thanks (casual to polite) |
| かんにん | ごめんなさい | Sorry, excuse me |
| ほんま | 本当 | Really, truly |
| えらい | とても、大変 | Very, terribly |
| あきまへん | だめです | No good |
| なんぼ | いくら | How much |

### Indirect Communication Patterns

| What They Say | What They May Mean |
|---------------|-------------------|
| 考えときます | Probably no |
| (Repeated tea offers) | Time to leave |
| ぶぶ漬けいかがどす | Please go home |
| また来てください | Polite dismissal (context-dependent) |

## Pedagogical Patterns

### Level-Appropriate Introduction

| Level | Standard JP | Kyoto-ben Focus |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Beginner | Foundation | Recognition only, おおきに |
| Intermediate | Past/future, opinions | はる recognition, expressions |
| Advanced | Nuance, register | はる production, cultural depth |

### Common Learner Errors

| Error | Issue | Guidance |
|-------|-------|----------|
| はる for own actions | はる is for others | Use regular forms for self |
| Overusing どす | Sounds archaic/theatrical | Use です normally |
| Mixing Osaka-ben | Different dialects | Note differences when they arise |

## Cultural Context

### Historical Significance
- Kyoto as former imperial capital
- Association with traditional arts (tea ceremony, geisha culture)
- Reputation for refinement and indirectness

### Modern Usage
- Younger generations use less dialect
- はる remains common across ages
- Traditional expressions more common in certain contexts
